Andra King Taluka - 110.4 million & BOX OFFICE COLLECTION 🤗

Andhra King Taluka is a Telugu romantic entertainer movie directed by Ms. Shetty Mr. Polishetty movie fame P Mahesh Babu. The movie casts Ram Pothineni and Bhagyashri Borse in the main lead. The music was composed by Vivek - Marvin while the film is produced by Naveen Yerneni and Y. Ravi Shankar under Mythri Movie Makers banner.

Actors:Ram Pothineni

Actress:BhagyaSree

8 Vasanthalu - 100 million & BOX OFFICE COLLECTION

Shuddhi Ayodhya (Ananthika Sanilkumar) is a young writer and martial artist raised by her mother in Ooty after the death of her army father. With clarity, courage and creative talent, she pours her grief into writing and emerges as a rising literary voice. When Varun (Hanu Reddy) walks into her life, love briefly blooms before heartbreak sets her on a path of self-reinvention. As Shuddhi moves through these emotional seasons, the film explores how each “spring” transforms her.

Actor:Ravi Dugirala

Actress:Ananthika SanilKumar

Director:Phanindra Narsetti

Seethamma Vakitlo Sirimalle Chettu - 50 million & BOX OFFICE COLLECTION

The story of ‘Seethamma Vakitlo Sirimalle Chettu’ is set in a small village called Relangi. The story revolves around a lovable family headed by Prakash Raj. Fondly called as Relangi Mavayya by his nephews and nieces, Prakash Raj is seen as a kind hearted gentleman who gives great importance to human values and relationships. He has two sons – Peddodu (Venkatesh) and Chinnodu (Mahesh Babu). Venkatesh is seen as a strong willed and highly emotional guy who does not like to bow down to others. He believes in doing what is right and waits for the ‘big golden chance’ in life. He is unemployed, but very honest and loves his younger brother Mahesh a lot. Seetha (Anjali) is his maradalu. She is very attached to Venkatesh and grows up believing him to be her husband.

Actors:Mahesh Babu,Venkatesh

Actress:Samantha,Anjali

Director:Srikanth Addala

Kamali From Nadukkaveri - 40 Million & BOX OFFICE COLLECTION

The synopsis of Kamali From Nadukkaveri gives an impression that the film is about a girl who falls into the “loosu ponnu” (which Kollywood viewers can easily identify) category. However, director Rajasekar ensures that this female-centric film has its soul in the right place. Kamali (Anandhi), a happy-go-lucky girl from a small town, is introduced as someone who indulges in a mischievous act everyday on the way to her school.Kamali’s life begins to change as soon as she falls in love with a studious boy in Chennai who aspires to make it to IIT Madras. She decides to take her studies seriously and convinces her father to go for higher studies. With the help of a retired professor (Pratap Pothen) in her village, she starts preparing for exams to join IIT Madras, only to meet her love. Quite strangely, the Chennai-based ambitious boy is clueless about a girl from a village wanting to join the prestigious institution with the sole aim of meeting him.

Actor:Rohith saraf

Actress:Anandi

Director:Prathap.k
